Our Caring Philosophy

Where Comfort Meets Exceptional Style.

Founded in 2014 by certified master groomers, Blackberry Luxury Grooming was built on a simple premise: dogs deserve a peaceful, enjoyable grooming experience without harsh cages or hurried treatments.

100% Hypoallergenic & Organic

We exclusively use eco-friendly, sulfate-free shampoos formulated for sensitive canine skin.

Fear-Free Certified Handling

Our staff is trained in positive reinforcement techniques to calm nervous or rescue pets.

Private Quiet Suites

Individual relaxing lounge areas instead of loud, stressful cage banks.
